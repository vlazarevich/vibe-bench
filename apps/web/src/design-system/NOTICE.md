Inter, Manrope and JetBrains Mono are bundled locally from Google Fonts, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and license notices are in `licenses/`.

Codex, Claude and OpenCode marks identify their respective harnesses. They remain the property of their vendors and retain the colors and proportions supplied by the handoff.
